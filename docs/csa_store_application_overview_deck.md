# CSA Store Application Overview Deck

## Slide 1. Title
- CSA Store
- One platform for operations, subscriptions, pricing, and analytics

## Slide 2. Current Situation
- Work is split across Wix, Zapier, Local Line, scripts, and Google Sheets.
- Data lives in several places.
- Reporting is patched together.
- Attribution is weak.
- Laura spends time managing handoffs instead of one clean workflow.

## Slide 3. What CSA Store Does Now
- Pulls Local Line products, orders, fulfillments, and subscriber snapshots into one admin.
- Controls pricing from one place.
- Tracks drop-site performance and order trends.
- Publishes dashboards from local data.
- Runs a local subscribe page with lead capture and nearest-site guidance.
- Tracks subscription leads in admin.

## Slide 4. Operational Value Today
- Fewer manual handoffs.
- Better visibility into orders, subscribers, pricing, and drop sites.
- Stronger operational reporting.
- The app already acts as the control layer around Local Line, Wix, and reporting.

## Slide 5. What We Want It To Do Next
- Replace the remaining stitched-together workflow.
- Capture and convert subscription leads more efficiently.
- Match members to nearest drop site or delivery.
- Add campaign tracking and subscriber attribution.
- Add better retention and communication tools.
- Operate the system vertically on DigitalOcean with Codex-assisted iteration.

## Slide 6. Why A Single Interface Matters
- One source of truth.
- Less Wix, Zapier, script, and Sheet overhead.
- Faster changes.
- Better pricing control.
- Better conversion tracking.
- Less manual work for Laura.

## Slide 7. ROI: Direct Cost Savings
- Current annual tool and support cost: about $10,000
- Target annual maintenance: about $5,000
- Direct annual savings: about $5,000
- Additional labor savings come from less admin work and fewer manual handoffs.

## Slide 8. ROI: Conversion And Retention Upside
- About 150 members leave each year.
- About 150 new members join each year.
- A better portal improves intake and retention.
- That yields about 30 additional members per year.
- Estimated annual value: about $72,000.

## Slide 9. ROI: Pricing And Margin Control
- Pricing control creates profit, not just labor savings.
- Even a 2% markup improvement is worth more than $10,000 per year.
- Value comes from cleaner formula pricing and tighter review.

## Slide 10. ROI: Marketing And Attribution
- Today, marketing attribution is weak.
- The planned module would connect campaigns, locations, subscriptions, and spend.
- That means better decisions on where to spend and what to promote.
- This upside is real and not included in the base ROI math.

## Slide 11. Annual Value Summary
- Direct cost savings: about $5,000
- Conversion and retention upside: about $72,000
- Pricing and margin upside: more than $10,000
- Total identified annual value before marketing upside: more than $87,000
- Compared to about $5,000 annual maintenance, this is a very strong ROI case.

## Slide 12. End Goal
- Replace the scotch-tape ecosystem.
- Run subscriptions, pricing, analytics, workflows, and marketing in one place.
- Own the platform.
- Improve it continuously with Codex-assisted iteration.
