What was preserved

Vendor defaults, base prices, inventory, tracking, visibility, descriptions, images, and sales were not changed by this operation. No Square or turkey preorder updates were sent. The markup is saved as an explicit product override; defaults inherited by newly created products remain unchanged. Only existing Local Line price-list entries were updated, and no missing listings were created.

For a product whose customer price previously reflected 40%, moving to 45% raises the unrounded customer price by about 3.57%. Existing app rounding is preserved, including the half-cent behavior seen in the hazelnut trial. 20 products had pre-existing Local Line rates or fixed prices that differed from local 40% pricing; the workbook records those values. Local Line displayed-price figures in this report are calculated from verified API inputs rather than a public checkout.
