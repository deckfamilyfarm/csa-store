  • Local prices are the saved active enterprise pricing revision, including saved sale settings. Missing local inputs are not inferred from cached vendor Cost or remote values.
  • Square prices are USD rates for one lb or one each/package. Local FF member and Local Line prices are per package unless Local Line basis explicitly says weight-based.
  • Local Line FF member means the active Herd Share Members basket (2966); retired CSA Members (2718) is excluded. Guest Basket (3124) and SNAP (5622) are separate observed live prices.
  • Local Line fixed basket prices use the live adjustment value; other adjustments are calculated from the live API package price and adjustment. This is not the vendor Cost/base field.
  • Square selling prices use Orders Calculate with automatic discounts enabled, one unit per linked variation, and taxes disabled. No order was created. Every active location was checked; main and Hillsdale prices are shown explicitly.
  • Different Square/Local Line parent names can be intentional shared-item mappings. Names and labels are shown for review, never automatically relinked.
  • This comparison is read-only: no products, prices, links, exclusions, sale settings, stock, or remote visibility were changed.
