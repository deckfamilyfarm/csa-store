SHOP DECISION

The existing Wix shop and its product IDs are accessible through the API, so a
new shop is not required for catalog discovery. Printify documents support for
extending existing sales-channel integrations through its API:
https://developers.printify.com/

Reusing the existing product records is a candidate for preserving production
details, especially the non-media layers. Direct order submission, production
approval policy and channel bookkeeping have not been exercised. The retrieved
shop list does not expose approval settings. Before choosing fulfillment through
this shop, review how test/manual approval would coexist with Wix orders. A
separate API shop remains an option if isolation is needed; copying is not safe
to assume for every legacy product. Do not change the Wix approval policy as an
implicit part of testing. Do not disconnect Wix or remove source products.
